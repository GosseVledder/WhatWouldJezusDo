What Would Jezus Do

Privacy policy

Applies to the What Would Jezus Do browser extension (Chrome, Edge) and Android app. Effective 2026-11-03.

In short

What Would Jezus Do has no server of its own and does not collect, sell or share data about you. It runs on your device and talks directly to the services listed below, using API keys that you create yourself. There is no analytics, tracking or advertising.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your API keys. In the extension they are kept in the extension's own IndexedDB storage, which YouTube pages cannot read. In the Android app they are stored encrypted with the Android Keystore; Android backups are switched off for the app.
  • Your settings, such as language and display options.
  • Analyses you ran (scores, summaries, checked statements and their sources), so that opening the same video again needs no new AI call. You can clear them in Settings.
  • An optional debug log (off by default): per AI call the model, duration and token counts. It never contains your keys.

What is sent, and to whom

  • YouTube (youtube.com): the app requests the transcript (captions) and basic details of the video you are watching or shared.
  • The AI service you choose: Google Gemini (default, generativelanguage.googleapis.com) or Anthropic Claude (api.anthropic.com). It receives the transcript, the video's title, channel and publication date, the analysis instructions and your API key for that service. The service may use web search to check claims.
  • Google Fact Check Tools API (factchecktools.googleapis.com): short search terms for each claim, with your Fact Check key.
  • GitHub (api.github.com, github.com): only when you click "Check for updates", to look up and download the latest version. No keys or other data are sent.

These services process the data under their own terms and privacy policies, as part of your own account with them: Google (policies.google.com/privacy, and the Gemini API terms) and Anthropic (anthropic.com/legal/privacy). Note that on Google's free (unpaid) Gemini tier, Google's terms say it uses the content you submit and the responses to improve its products, and that human reviewers may read it. WWJD sends the video's transcript and details, not personal data about you; if this matters to you, use a paid Gemini key or Claude.

What the app does not do

  • It does not read your YouTube account, history or other tabs and websites.
  • It does not send data to the developer.
  • It does not use your data for advertising, profiling or credit decisions.

Permissions (extension)

  • storage, unlimitedStorage: settings and saved analyses on your device.
  • declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess: lets the extension's own transcript requests to youtube.com be accepted by YouTube.
  • Access to youtube.com (the panel and badges), and to the Gemini, Claude, Fact Check and GitHub API addresses listed above.

Your choices

You can delete saved analyses and your keys in Settings at any time, or remove the extension or app to delete everything it stored. Keys can also be revoked at Google or Anthropic.

Contact

Questions or problems: open an issue on GitHub. Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
