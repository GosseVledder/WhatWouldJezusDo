# What Would Jezus Do 0.4.1

Extension 0.4.1 (Chrome, Edge) · Android app 0.4.1 (versionCode 14)

## Clearer substantiation

- **Two rubrics: *Story & facts* and *Presenter*.** The substantiation is split into two clearly
  marked parts, each with its own score and colour. Everything about the story (summary, checked
  facts, fact checks per statement, the evaluation of the subject) is under *Story & facts*;
  the presenter's role, conduct, attitude, own contradictions and score are under *Presenter*.
  When the presenter is the subject (a vlog, sermon or speech) there is one part, as before.
- **Filter.** Show *All*, only *Story & facts* or only the *Presenter* with one tap. The choice
  is kept while you keep using the page or app. Synced live mode does not scroll to hidden parts.
- **Checked facts as a table**: time, claim and the verdict in its own column on the right.

## Progress you can follow

- The step that is running shows a **running clock**, finished steps show **how long they took**.
- After a first analysis the list also shows **how long a step usually takes** ("usually ~40 s"),
  learned from your last five analyses with the same AI model on this device.
